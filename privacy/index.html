PRIVACY POLICY

Effective November 19, 2026

Summary

Courtline shows NBA scores, stats and fan votes. You can use it without an account. We collect as little as we can, we do not sell your data, and we do not show personalized ads.

What we collect

An anonymous ID. A random code created on your phone the first time you open the app. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.

What you choose to share. Your votes and picks, the display name you set, the team you follow, and any feedback you send. These are stored with your anonymous ID so vote counts and the leaderboard work.

An account, only if you sign in. Signing in is optional. If you sign in with email, we store your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We use these only to keep your votes and picks with you across devices. We never see your Google password.

Technical data. Like any internet service, our servers receive your IP address and basic request details when the app downloads scores. We use them only to deliver the service and keep it secure.

What stays on your phone

Saved scores, schedules, pictures, alert settings and recent votes are kept on your device so the app opens instantly. Alerts are created on your phone; we do not use a push service.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only. Google may still collect device information to show and measure ads; see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. Ad-free subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

Ad-free subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a subscription is active, never your card or payment details.

Service providers

Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and servers. Cloudflare, GitHub and jsDelivr deliver public score data, which contains no personal information. OpenAI helps write match previews and articles from game statistics only; no personal data is sent to it. Google provides sign-in (if you choose it), ads and purchases.

How long we keep data

Votes, picks and your display name are kept for the current and previous season, then deleted or made anonymous. Account details are kept until you delete your account. Feedback is kept for up to two years.

Your choices and rights

You can change your display name and team at any time, sign out, turn alerts off, and ask us to delete your account and everything linked to it. See Delete your data. You can reset what is stored on your phone by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our database is restricted.

Children

Courtline is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact us through the support email shown on Courtline's Google Play listing, or the Feedback form in the app (More → Send feedback).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
