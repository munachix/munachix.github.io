Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Wanderer stores only what the quest loop needs. We do not sell personal information, and we do not show ads.

Information we collect

This policy covers the Wanderer mobile app and this website. Contact: wandererapp.support@gmail.com.

  • Account details: email address, password (stored hashed by our sign-in provider), username, display name, and the profile photo and bio you choose to add.
  • Location: your device's precise location while you look for quests, verify a quest, or run a walk quest you started. Used to find places near you and to confirm a visit.
  • Photos: photos you take in the app to verify a quest, with the time and location stored in the photo.
  • Activity: quests offered and completed, Explorer Rating, streaks, badges, quests you create, people you follow and kudos you give.
  • Purchases: the subscription product and store receipt token for Wanderer Pro. Payment details are handled by Google Play and never reach us.
  • Feedback: messages you send from the app.

We use this information only to run Wanderer: to create and secure your account, generate quests near you, verify completions, show leaderboards, your profile and your friends' activity, provide Pro, answer feedback, and protect the service from cheating and abuse. We do not sell personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data to train AI models.

Google user data

If you choose Sign in with Google, Wanderer requests only the basic sign-in scopes (openid, email and profile). From Google we receive your email address, name, profile picture and a Google account identifier.

  • How we use it: to create your Wanderer account, sign you in, and fill in an empty display name or profile photo. Nothing else.
  • What we don't access: Gmail, contacts, Google Drive, Calendar, Photos or any other Google data. We never see your Google password.
  • Sharing: we don't sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, except our hosting provider (Supabase), which stores it on our behalf to run sign-in.
  • Advertising and AI: Google user data is never used for advertising, and never used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.
  • Retention and deletion: kept while your account exists. Deleting your account (see Delete your account) removes it after 14 days. You can also remove Wanderer's access at any time in your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps and services.

Wanderer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Account

Email, password, username, display name, and bio live in our database so you can sign in and so other explorers can see the public profile you chose to share.

Location

When you generate or verify a quest, we use your live position to find nearby places and to check that you were there. Exact capture coordinates from a verification are visible to you and to moderators reviewing that photo. They are not placed on the public leaderboard.

Photos

Verification photos are uploaded so a quest can be checked. Moderators can open a photo that was sent for review. Photos are not used to train a public model.

Purchases

A Pro trial is recorded on your account. A store subscription is confirmed with Google Play or the App Store. Wanderer keeps the product id and the receipt token so it can tell whether Pro is still active. The store handles the charge.

What you can change

You can edit your name, username, bio, and home city, change your password, turn notifications on or off, sign out of this phone, and delete the account from Profile. Deleting the account removes your profile, quests, and photos 14 days later; signing back in before then keeps it. A store subscription has to be canceled in Google Play or the App Store.

Services that process data for us

Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and photo storage. Google Play handles payments; we never see your card. To build quests, your approximate position is sent from our servers to OpenStreetMap place services and to Open-Meteo for the local weather, without your name or email. In mainland China the app looks up nearby places with Tianditu from your phone. Photo checks such as recognising objects run on your phone.

On your phone

The app keeps a copy of recent screens on your phone so it opens quickly and works when the connection drops. If you add the home-screen widget, today's quest title and your streak are stored on the phone for it. Reminders are scheduled on the phone.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete the account, it is kept for 14 days in case you change your mind, then your profile, quests, photos, streak and rating are deleted. Purchase records may be kept as long as tax and store rules require.

Children

Wanderer is not for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each account read and change only its own private data.

Your rights

You can see and change your data in the app, and delete your account at any time (see Delete your account). You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and show the change in the app. Contact: wandererapp.support@gmail.com.
