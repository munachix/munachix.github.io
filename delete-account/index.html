Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your Courtline account and everything linked to it: your email address or Google sign-in, display name, votes, picks, leaderboard entries and feedback.

How to ask

Contact us through the support email shown on Courtline's Google Play listing, or the Feedback form in the app (More → Send feedback). Include the email address you signed in with, or, if you never signed in, your anonymous ID (in the app: More → Privacy policy, at the bottom of the page).

What happens next

We delete your account and linked data within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done. Vote totals that were already counted stay as anonymous numbers and cannot be traced back to you.

Delete only what is on your phone

Saved scores, pictures and settings live only on your device. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to remove them.
